  • Grad Diploma in Psychology, Monash University, Australia, BSc University of Alberta, Canada

With over 20 years of experience working with families and children, I support parents in building strong, emotionally connected relationships. My work across Singapore, Malaysia, and Nepal has helped families grow through understanding behavior, emotions, and conscious parenting.

My Story, My Why

I wasn’t always the parent I am today.

Like many, I believed control meant safety and strictness meant love. I followed the patterns I had grown up with, trusting they were right.

I didn’t have the words, but with time I knew something was missing. Our connection was slowly fading.”.

That was my turning point. That discomfort changed everything.

I realized:
Nothing was wrong with me as a parent.
I just needed awareness… and support. I moved from Control to Connection

I  replaced :

  • Control with Connection
  • Fear with Curiosity
  • Judgement with Understanding

Today, I help parents experience this shift so that their  children feel

safe, seen, and deeply connected
